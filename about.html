Background

Strategy with creative consequences.

I’ve spent my career helping companies clarify what they stand for, what they should say, how they should show up, and how teams can make better decisions before they spend money making the wrong thing.

I come from the client side, the agency side, and the classroom. I also teach AI for Advertising and creative strategy at the graduate and undergraduate level at the Academy of Art University in San Francisco, which keeps my work grounded in how fast the tools and expectations are actually evolving. That combination matters. It means I care about business reality, creative quality, and whether people can actually use the system after the meeting ends.

Today, most teams do not have an ideas problem. They have a clarity problem, a workflow problem, and increasingly, an AI orchestration problem. The tools are powerful. The system around them is usually not.

That is the space I work in: helping teams find the strategy, shape the creative, and build the modern workflows required to move with more confidence.
